TECH POLICY: PASSWORDS AND AUTHENTICATION

1. Single Sign-On (SSO)
All employees must use the corporate SSO provider to access internal tools and the SaaS product.
SSO logins require multi-factor authentication (MFA) via mobile app or hardware token.
Shared accounts are prohibited. Each user must have an individual identity.

2. Password Requirements
For systems that do not support SSO, passwords must be at least 14 characters long.
Passwords must include at least three of the following categories: lowercase, uppercase, digits, symbols.
Password reuse across personal and corporate systems is not allowed.

3. Account Lockouts
Accounts are locked after 5 failed login attempts within 15 minutes.
Locked accounts automatically unlock after 30 minutes, or can be reset by the helpdesk.
When users are locked out of their account, they should open an IT ticket titled "Account locked".

4. VPN Access
VPN is required to access restricted internal resources such as databases and admin panels.
VPN access requires an active employee account and MFA.
VPN performance issues should be triaged using the VPN runbook.

5. Device Security
Company laptops must use full-disk encryption and automatic screen lock after 5 minutes of inactivity.
Installing unapproved software is prohibited. Requests for new tools must be submitted via the IT request form.
Security incidents, such as suspected malware, must be reported to security@company.example immediately.

6. Credential Sharing Prohibition
Employees must not share passwords, MFA tokens, or authentication codes with anyone, including colleagues and managers.
Support staff will never ask for a password and will only provide secure reset links.
Suspected credential compromise should be reported immediately to security@company.example.

7. Authentication for Third-Party Tools
Where possible, third-party tools must be integrated with corporate SSO instead of using separate login credentials.
Service accounts or API keys must follow the company’s secrets management guidelines and be stored securely.
Access reviews for third-party tools should be conducted regularly in partnership with security.

8. Lost or Stolen Devices
If a device is lost or stolen, the employee must inform IT and security without delay.
Remote wipe, account lock, and password resets may be initiated to protect company data.
A short incident report may be required to document what happened and what data might be at risk.

9. Temporary Access Requests
Temporary or emergency access to privileged systems must follow the approved access request workflow.
Such access should be time-limited and logged, with justifications documented in the ticketing system.
After the temporary period, elevated permissions must be revoked.

10. Policy Compliance and Training
Employees must complete periodic security and authentication training modules.
Repeated or severe violations of this policy may lead to disciplinary action.
The security team reviews this policy regularly and will notify staff of material changes.
