# TECH POLICY: PASSWORDS AND AUTHENTICATION

## 1. Single Sign-On (SSO)
All employees must use the corporate SSO provider to access internal tools and the SaaS product.
SSO logins require multi-factor authentication (MFA) via mobile app or hardware token.
Shared accounts are prohibited. Each user must have an individual identity.

## 2. Password Requirements
For systems that do not support SSO, passwords must be at least 14 characters long.
Passwords must include at least three of the following categories: lowercase, uppercase, digits, symbols.
Password reuse across personal and corporate systems is not allowed.

## 3. Account Lockouts
Accounts are locked after 5 failed login attempts within 15 minutes.
Locked accounts automatically unlock after 30 minutes, or can be reset by the helpdesk.
When users are locked out of their account, they should open an IT ticket titled "Account locked".

## 4. VPN Access
VPN is required to access restricted internal resources such as databases and admin panels.
VPN access requires an active employee account and MFA.
VPN performance issues should be triaged using the VPN runbook.

## 5. Device Security
Company laptops must use full-disk encryption and automatic screen lock after 5 minutes of inactivity.
Installing unapproved software is prohibited. Requests for new tools must be submitted via the IT request form.
Security incidents, such as suspected malware, must be reported to security@company.example immediately.
