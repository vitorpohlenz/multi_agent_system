FINANCE POLICY: PRICING AND DISCOUNTS

1. Standard Pricing
Standard subscription pricing is published on the public website.
Custom enterprise pricing is available for large customers subject to approval.

2. Discounts
Discounts above 20% off list price require approval from the Finance team.
Multi-year agreements may justify higher discounts in exchange for longer commitments.

3. Billing Frequency Changes
Customers may request to change from monthly to annual billing at the next renewal date.
Mid-term changes must be reviewed by Finance and may require a contract amendment.
Switching from annual to monthly is generally not allowed mid-term.

4. Taxes
Customers are responsible for providing valid tax exemption certificates where applicable.
If no certificate is provided, applicable VAT or sales tax will be charged.

5. Promotional Offers
Limited-time promotional offers or trial discounts must be documented and approved before being presented to customers.
The terms of the promotion, including duration and eligibility, must be clearly stated in the order form or agreement.
Once a promotion expires, standard pricing applies unless otherwise agreed in writing.

6. Volume-Based Pricing
For customers purchasing large volumes or multiple products, volume-based pricing tiers may apply.
Sales must consult Finance before offering non-standard volume discounts to ensure profitability.
Any custom pricing agreements must be recorded in the contract and internal systems.

7. Currency and FX Considerations
For multi-currency deals, Finance will determine the billing currency based on contractual terms and operational feasibility.
Foreign exchange fluctuations are generally borne by the customer unless hedging arrangements are explicitly agreed.
Requests to change billing currency mid-term require Finance and Legal review.

8. Price Increases
Price increases follow the terms of the Master Service Agreement and are typically applied at renewal.
Customers should be notified of price changes in advance, in line with the notice period defined in the contract.
Sales and Customer Success teams must align on messaging and supporting materials for price adjustments.

9. Exceptions and Approvals
Any deviations from standard pricing, discount thresholds, or billing rules require documented approval from Finance leadership.
Exception approvals should include justification, expected revenue impact, and time period.
Unapproved discounts or informal promises to customers are not binding on the company.

10. Policy Governance
The Finance team reviews pricing and discount policies at least annually.
Updates are communicated to Sales, Customer Success, and Legal to ensure consistent application.
Questions or edge cases should be escalated to the Finance leadership team for guidance.
