FINANCE POLICY: EMPLOYEE EXPENSES AND REIMBURSEMENT

1. Eligible Expenses
Eligible business expenses include travel, accommodation, meals with clients, and approved software tools.
Personal expenses, fines, and luxury items are not reimbursable.

2. Expense Submission
Employees must submit expenses via the expense management system within 30 days of incurring the cost.
Receipts are required for all expenses above the local petty cash threshold.

3. Approval Workflow
Expense reports are routed to the employee's manager for approval.
Finance audits expense reports regularly and may request additional documentation.

4. Reimbursement Timing
Reimbursements are processed with the next payroll cycle after approval.
Delays may occur if documentation is incomplete.

5. Travel Guidelines
Employees should book travel using approved vendors and follow the company’s travel class guidelines.
Where possible, employees are expected to choose cost-effective options that balance safety, comfort, and price.
Travel upgrades for personal preference are not reimbursable unless pre-approved.

6. Meal and Entertainment Expenses
Meals with clients or partners must have a documented business purpose, including attendees and topics discussed.
There may be per-person or per-day limits on meal expenses depending on region and role.
Purely social or personal meals are not considered reimbursable business expenses.

7. Home Office and Remote Work Expenses
Certain home office expenses, such as an ergonomic chair or external monitor, may be eligible if approved in advance.
Recurring home internet or phone costs may be reimbursed partially where required by local regulations or policy.
Employees should consult their manager and HR before making significant purchases.

8. Currency and Exchange Rates
Expenses incurred in a foreign currency will be converted based on the exchange rate used by the expense system or company card provider.
Employees should avoid unnecessary currency conversion fees where possible.
Any significant discrepancies due to rate differences can be reviewed by Finance.

9. Non-Compliant Expenses
Expenses that do not meet policy requirements may be rejected or partially reimbursed.
Repeated submission of non-compliant expenses may be escalated to HR or the employee’s manager for discussion.
Employees are encouraged to ask Finance for clarification before incurring borderline expenses.

10. Policy Updates
Expense policies may change to reflect new regulations, tax rules, or business priorities.
Updated guidelines will be published in the expense management system and communicated via internal channels.
Employees are responsible for reviewing the latest version of the policy before major trips or purchases.
