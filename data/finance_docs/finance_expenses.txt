# FINANCE POLICY: EMPLOYEE EXPENSES AND REIMBURSEMENT

## 1. Eligible Expenses
Eligible business expenses include travel, accommodation, meals with clients, and approved software tools.
Personal expenses, fines, and luxury items are not reimbursable.

## 2. Expense Submission
Employees must submit expenses via the expense management system within 30 days of incurring the cost.
Receipts are required for all expenses above the local petty cash threshold.

## 3. Approval Workflow
Expense reports are routed to the employee's manager for approval.
Finance audits expense reports regularly and may request additional documentation.

## 4. Reimbursement Timing
Reimbursements are processed with the next payroll cycle after approval.
Delays may occur if documentation is incomplete.
