# FINANCE FAQ: COMMON QUESTIONS

## Q1: How can I get a copy of my last invoice?
A1: Email billing@company.example or access the "Billing" section of the admin portal to download past invoices.

## Q2: Can I change my billing frequency from monthly to annual?
A2: Yes, customers may switch to annual billing at renewal. Mid-term changes require a review from Finance.

## Q3: How do I submit an expense report?
A3: Log in to the expense management system, create a new report, attach receipts, and submit for manager approval.

## Q4: When will I be reimbursed for expenses?
A4: Approved expenses are reimbursed in the next payroll cycle.
