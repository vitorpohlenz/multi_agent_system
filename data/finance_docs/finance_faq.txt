FINANCE FAQ: COMMON QUESTIONS

Q1: How can I get a copy of my last invoice?
A1: Email billing@company.example or access the "Billing" section of the admin portal to download past invoices.

Q2: Can I change my billing frequency from monthly to annual?
A2: Yes, customers may switch to annual billing at renewal. Mid-term changes require a review from Finance.

Q3: How do I submit an expense report?
A3: Log in to the expense management system, create a new report, attach receipts, and submit for manager approval.

Q4: When will I be reimbursed for expenses?
A4: Approved expenses are reimbursed in the next payroll cycle.

Q5: Who should I contact if I think my invoice is incorrect?
A5: Contact billing@company.example with the invoice number and a brief description of the issue. Finance will review and respond.

Q6: Can Finance help me understand the taxes on my invoice?
A6: Yes. Finance can explain how taxes were calculated based on your location and the information on file. Provide your tax ID and invoice number for faster support.

Q7: Are late payment fees applied automatically?
A7: Late payment reminders are typically sent automatically after the due date. Additional fees or service suspension follow the terms in your contract.

Q8: Can I pay by credit card instead of bank transfer?
A8: Many customers can pay by credit card using the payment link provided with the invoice, depending on region and contract.

Q9: How do I update the billing contact for my account?
A9: An authorized representative should email billing@company.example with the new contact details, including name, email address, and role.

Q10: Does Finance provide reports on total spend per year?
A10: Yes. Upon request, Finance can provide summary reports of invoiced amounts and payments received for a given period, subject to internal reporting timelines.
