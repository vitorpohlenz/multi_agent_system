FINANCE POLICY: BILLING AND INVOICING

1. Billing Cycles
Customers may choose monthly or annual billing for subscriptions.
Invoices are issued at the beginning of the billing period and sent by email to the billing contact.

2. Payment Terms
Standard payment terms are net 30 days from the invoice date.
Late payments are subject to reminder notices and potential suspension of service after 45 days.

3. Invoice Format
Invoices include the customer name, billing address, tax identification, subscription plan, unit price, quantity, and applicable taxes.
Currency is determined by the contract and cannot be changed mid-term without an amendment.

4. Refund Policy
Refunds are granted only in accordance with the Master Service Agreement.
Partial refunds may be issued for prolonged outages or billing errors, subject to approval by Finance.

5. Billing Support
Customers can contact billing@company.example for invoice copies, PO numbers, and general billing inquiries.

6. Dispute Resolution
If a customer believes there is an error on an invoice, they must notify Finance within 30 days of the invoice date.
Finance will investigate the dispute, request any necessary documentation, and communicate the outcome in writing.
Service suspension due to non-payment may be delayed while a good-faith dispute is under active review.

7. Credit Notes
When overbilling is confirmed, Finance may issue a credit note that can be applied to future invoices.
The credit note will specify the reason and the amount credited, referencing the original invoice.
Credit notes do not typically result in immediate cash refunds unless required by contract or law.

8. Billing Contact Changes
Customers are responsible for keeping their billing contact information up to date.
Requests to change the billing contact must come from an authorized representative and be documented in the CRM or contract file.
Out-of-date contact details that cause missed invoices do not exempt customers from payment obligations.

9. Tax Compliance
Invoices will include applicable taxes based on the customer’s location and the information provided.
Customers must supply valid tax identification numbers and exemption certificates where required.
Finance may adjust tax calculations if regulations change or if new information is provided.

10. Policy Review
This billing and invoicing policy may be updated periodically to reflect changes in regulations or business practices.
Material changes will be communicated to relevant internal stakeholders such as Sales, Customer Success, and Support.
Questions about this policy can be directed to the Finance team or billing@company.example.
