# FINANCE POLICY: BILLING AND INVOICING

## 1. Billing Cycles
Customers may choose monthly or annual billing for subscriptions.
Invoices are issued at the beginning of the billing period and sent by email to the billing contact.

## 2. Payment Terms
Standard payment terms are net 30 days from the invoice date.
Late payments are subject to reminder notices and potential suspension of service after 45 days.

## 3. Invoice Format
Invoices include the customer name, billing address, tax identification, subscription plan, unit price, quantity, and applicable taxes.
Currency is determined by the contract and cannot be changed mid-term without an amendment.

## 4. Refund Policy
Refunds are granted only in accordance with the Master Service Agreement.
Partial refunds may be issued for prolonged outages or billing errors, subject to approval by Finance.

## 5. Billing Support
Customers can contact billing@company.example for invoice copies, PO numbers, and general billing inquiries.
