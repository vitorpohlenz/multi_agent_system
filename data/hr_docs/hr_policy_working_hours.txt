# HR POLICY: WORKING HOURS AND ATTENDANCE

## 1. Standard Working Hours
Our standard working hours are 9:00 to 18:00 Monday–Friday with a one-hour unpaid lunch break.
Managers may approve flexible schedules as long as team coverage is maintained and business needs are met.
Employees must record their working hours in the official timekeeping system each day.

## 2. Remote Work
Remote work is permitted for eligible roles as defined in the role description.
Employees must be reachable through company communication tools (email, chat, and video) during agreed working hours.
Permanent remote work arrangements require a written agreement with HR and the line manager.

## 3. Overtime
Overtime must be approved in advance by the manager wherever practical.
Overtime is compensated according to local legislation and the employee's contract.
Employees are encouraged to manage their workload proactively and raise concerns early.

## 4. Attendance Issues
Employees who are late or unexpectedly absent must notify their manager as soon as possible.
Repeated unexcused absences may lead to a formal performance discussion and corrective action.
HR maintains records of formal warnings in accordance with privacy regulations.

## 5. Public Holidays
Public holidays are defined by the country of employment.
When a public holiday falls on a weekend, observance rules follow local regulations.
Employees should check the HR portal for the official calendar of holidays.

## Note: This policy applies to all regular employees, contractors, and interns unless stated otherwise.
For questions, contact hr@company.example.
