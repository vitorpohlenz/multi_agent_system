HR FAQ: COMMON QUESTIONS

Q1: How many vacation days do I have per year?
A1: Full-time employees receive 25 days of paid time off per calendar year, excluding public holidays. Part-time employees receive PTO on a pro-rated basis.

Q2: How do I check my remaining PTO balance?
A2: Log in to the HR self-service portal, navigate to "My Time Off," and your current balance and approved requests will be displayed.

Q3: How do I report workplace harassment or discrimination?
A3: You can report concerns to your manager, HR business partner, or via the anonymous ethics hotline. All complaints are handled confidentially and investigated promptly.

Q4: Can HR see my medical information?
A4: HR only stores the minimum medical information required by law, such as fit notes. Access is restricted to authorized HR staff.

Q5: What is the dress code?
A5: We have a "smart casual" dress code for the office. For external customer meetings, please coordinate with your manager on appropriate attire.

Q6: Who do I contact about payroll issues?
A6: For payroll questions, email payroll@company.example or raise a ticket in the HR portal under "Payroll and Compensation."

Q7: Can I work permanently from another country?
A7: Cross-border remote work is subject to tax, legal, and data protection requirements. Employees must obtain written approval from HR and Legal before relocating.

Q8: How do I update my personal information such as address or emergency contact?
A8: You can update most personal details directly in the HR self-service portal under "My Profile." For changes that cannot be edited, contact HR for assistance.

Q9: When are performance reviews conducted?
A9: Formal performance reviews are typically conducted once per year, with mid-year check-ins encouraged. Managers may schedule additional feedback sessions as needed.

Q10: Can I request a copy of my employment contract?
A10: Yes. You can request a copy of your signed employment contract and any contractual amendments by submitting a ticket to HR or emailing hr@company.example.
